\\# AtlasIQ







\\## Business Analytics, Anomaly Detection \\\& Predictive Analytics







AtlasIQ is an end-to-end business analytics and machine learning project designed to analyze business transactions, detect unusual activity, and identify factors influencing business outcomes.







\\## 🎯 Objectives







\\- Analyze business sales and profitability patterns



\\- Identify important business trends and relationships



\\- Detect unusual transactions using Isolation Forest



\\- Build and evaluate a predictive machine learning model



\\- Identify the most influential predictive features



\\- Convert analytical findings into actionable business recommendations







\\## 🛠️ Technology Stack







\\- Python



\\- Pandas



\\- NumPy



\\- Matplotlib



\\- Scikit-learn



\\- Jupyter Notebook



\\- Git \\\& GitHub







\\## 🔄 Project Workflow







\\### 1. Data Analysis







The dataset was cleaned, explored, and analyzed to understand revenue, quantity, pricing, discounts, and profitability patterns.







\\### 2. Exploratory Data Analysis







The project investigates relationships and trends across key business variables using statistical analysis and visualizations.







\\### 3. Anomaly Detection







Isolation Forest was used to identify transactions that differ significantly from normal transaction behavior.







Detected anomalies can indicate:







\\- Exceptional business activity



\\- Unusual pricing



\\- Unusual quantities



\\- Unexpected discounts



\\- Abnormal profitability



\\- Potential data-quality issues







\\### 4. Predictive Analytics







A machine learning model was developed to predict the selected business outcome.







Model performance was evaluated using:







\\- Mean Absolute Error (MAE)



\\- Root Mean Squared Error (RMSE)



\\- R² Score







Feature importance analysis was performed to identify the variables contributing most strongly to predictions.







\\## 📊 Generated Reports







AtlasIQ generates analytical outputs including:







\\- `detected\\\_anomalies.csv`



\\- `feature\\\_importance.csv`



\\- `revenue\\\_predictions.csv`







These reports provide structured outputs that can be used for further investigation and decision-making.







\\## 💡 Business Insights







The analysis helps identify:







\\- Important revenue and profitability patterns



\\- Unusual transactions requiring investigation



\\- Variables strongly associated with predicted business outcomes



\\- Areas where pricing, discounts, quantity, or profitability should be monitored







\\## 🚀 Business Recommendations







1\\. Regularly monitor anomalous transactions.



2\\. Investigate unusual pricing, quantity, revenue, and profitability patterns.



3\\. Monitor the most influential predictive features.



4\\. Evaluate discount strategies against profitability.



5\\. Use predictive analytics to support business planning and decision-making.
