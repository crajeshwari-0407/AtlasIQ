# AtlasIQ

## Business Analytics, Anomaly Detection & Predictive Analytics

AtlasIQ is an end-to-end business analytics and machine learning project designed to analyze business transactions, identify unusual activity, discover important business patterns, and support predictive decision-making.

The project combines Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Scikit-learn, and Jupyter Notebook to transform raw business transaction data into analytical insights, anomaly reports, predictive outputs, and actionable business recommendations.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

- Business sales and revenue analysis
- Profitability analysis
- Quantity and pricing analysis
- Discount impact analysis
- Exploratory data analysis
- Business trend identification
- Transaction-level anomaly detection
- Isolation Forest based anomaly detection
- Predictive revenue analytics
- Machine learning model evaluation
- Feature importance analysis
- Automated analytical reports
- Business-oriented interpretation of ML results
- Pandas-based data processing

## 📊 Business Analytics Modules

AtlasIQ analyzes multiple dimensions of business performance.

### Revenue Analysis

The project analyzes transaction-level revenue to understand sales patterns and identify factors associated with business revenue.

Key areas include:

- Revenue distribution
- Revenue trends
- Transaction-level revenue
- Product-level performance
- Revenue-driving factors

---

### Profitability Analysis

AtlasIQ evaluates business profitability using transaction-level financial information.

The analysis helps identify:

- High-profit transactions
- Low-profit transactions
- Negative-profit transactions
- Profitability patterns
- Relationships between revenue and profit

---

### Pricing & Quantity Analysis

The project examines how pricing and quantity influence business outcomes.

Analysis includes:

- Unit price patterns
- Quantity distribution
- Revenue generated by quantity
- Pricing and profitability relationships
- Unusual pricing or quantity behavior

---

### Discount Analysis

Discounts are analyzed to understand their relationship with revenue and profitability.

The analysis helps identify:

- High-discount transactions
- Discount-related revenue patterns
- Discount and profitability relationships
- Transactions requiring further investigation

---

## 🚨 Anomaly Detection

AtlasIQ uses the **Isolation Forest** machine learning algorithm to identify transactions that differ significantly from normal business behavior.

The anomaly detection process considers important transaction characteristics such as:

- Revenue
- Quantity
- Pricing
- Discounts
- Profitability

Detected anomalies may represent:

- Exceptional business activity
- Unusual pricing
- Unusual quantities
- Unexpected discounts
- Abnormal profitability
- Potential data-quality issues
- Transactions requiring further investigation
